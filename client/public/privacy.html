Privacy Policy

Pose Navigator · Last updated 30 August 2026

Pose Navigator has no accounts, no sign-in, and no tracking. It does not collect, store, or transmit personal information about you. There is no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking software of any kind in the app.

What stays on your device

Two things are saved locally, in the app's own storage on your phone:

  • Your saved poses — the poses you star.
  • An offline copy of the pose library — poses, their relationships, and the written explanations, so the app works without a network connection.

Neither is ever transmitted anywhere. We cannot see your saved poses. They are not backed up to us, synced between your devices, or shared with anyone. Deleting the app deletes them.

What the app requests over the network

To show you pose content, the app requests it from posenavigator.com, and pose images from our image storage provider. These are ordinary web requests. We do not send any identifier with them — no account, no device ID, no advertising identifier.

As with any website, our hosting and storage providers automatically record standard technical request logs, which can include an IP address, for security and reliability. We do not use these logs to build a profile of you, and we do not combine them with anything else.

The app's fonts are bundled inside the app itself, so displaying it makes no request to any font or content delivery network.

What we do not do

  • No analytics or usage measurement
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers
  • No third-party SDKs, trackers, or social media integrations
  • No selling or sharing of data — we have none to sell
  • No location, contacts, camera, microphone, photo, or health data access

Children

Pose Navigator is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone. Because the app collects no personal information at all, it holds nothing about a child or an adult.

Health and safety

Pose Navigator is an educational reference about yoga postures and how they relate to one another. It is not medical advice and is not a substitute for a qualified teacher or healthcare professional. Cautions shown alongside a pose are general guidance, not an assessment of your body. Practise within your limits, and stop if something hurts.

Changes

If this policy changes, the revised version will be posted at this address with a new date above. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
